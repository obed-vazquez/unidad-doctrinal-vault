There are 3 type of teaching in the administration of the Lord's Supper referring to its participants.

1. **Open TableCommunion**.  The church allows that any person take the Holy Supper.  
2. Denominational or **Close TableCommunion**. Only people who are members of a church with the same denomination can join to take the Holy Supper.  
3. **Closed TableCommunion**. Only the local church can take the Lord's Supper.

In here the 3 of them are being analyzed but the analysis will focus on the of the doctrine of open communion.
